User manual

What is PYM Split?

PYM Split is an app to split expenses...

1. Create an account

On the home screen, tap Create account...

After registering, check your email...

2. Complete your Profile

Tap the Profile button at the top right...

  • Edit your name.
  • Add your phone (optional).
  • Choose your preferred currency.
  • Choose the app language.

The preferred currency defines...

3. Add friends

Tap the Friends button at the top right.

  • Type your friend email and tap Send.
  • Your friend will receive a request...
  • You can also accept or reject requests...

Note: your friend must be registered...

4. Create a group

On the home screen, tap + New above My Groups.

  • Name: e.g. Trip to Cancun...
  • Type: trip, apartment, outings or other.
  • Currency: the group main currency.
  • Start and end date (optional).
  • Manual rate (optional).

Tap Create Group and that is it...

5. Add friends to the group

Open the group and tap + Add member.

Select one of your friends from the list...

6. Add an expense

Tap the + button (bottom right).

  • What was it: e.g. Dinner, Taxi...
  • Amount: how much it cost.
  • Group: which group it belongs to.
  • Who paid: who put in the money.
  • Currency: in which currency it was paid.
  • Category: food, transport, etc.
  • Split type: how it is divided.

After saving, the main dashboard updates...

7. Split types

  • Equal parts: the total is divided equally...
  • By percentage: you assign a % to each...
  • Exact amounts: you enter the exact amount...
  • By shares: e.g. one has 2 shares...

You can check or uncheck people...

8. Multiple currencies

Each group has its main currency...

Example: a group in MXN...

In your Profile you choose your preferred currency...

9. Automatic and manual rates

The app tries to get the rate from 2 automatic sources:

  • Frankfurter (European Central Bank): EUR, USD, MXN, BRL, CLP, etc.
  • DolarAPI (Argentina): ARS, and other Latin American currencies.

If the currency is not in either of them...

Important: the manual rate is always saved against USD...

10. Edit the manual rate

If a group has a manual rate, the Edit rate button appears...

When you tap it, you can change the value...

11. View the group balance

When you open a group, the Balance appears at the top...

The app simplifies debts: if A owes B...

12. Trip total and My share

In the group details, above the balance, 2 numbers are shown:

  • Trip total: how much was spent in total.
  • My share: how much corresponds to you...

If your preferred currency differs from the group...

13. Calculate in today rate

In the details of an archived group...

  • The total spent with the rates at the time...
  • The total it would cost with today rates.
  • The difference (how much it went up or down).

It is informative only.

14. Close the calculation of a trip

When the trip ends, tap Close calculation...

  • The closing date (automatic, not editable).
  • The final total of the trip in the group currency.

If you made a mistake, you can tap Reopen...

This is useful to leave the trip result frozen.

15. Settle debts

When someone has already paid outside...

You confirm and the balance updates automatically.

Note: the app does NOT move money...

16. Main dashboard

The home screen shows:

  • Range tabs: Active groups / Archived groups / All.
  • 3 cards: Total spent, They owe you, You owe...
  • Recent activity: the 5 most recent expenses.
  • See history button: opens the chart + table...

All amounts are automatically converted...

17. Monthly history

Tap the See history button...

A modal opens with:

  • Bar chart: how much was spent each month.
  • Table: month, number of expenses and total.
  • Final row: total of the last 12 months.

18. Group history and charts

Inside each group, further down, you will see...

  • Charts: how much each person paid...
  • Totals by category: breakdown...
  • History: all movements...

Tap it again to hide them...

19. Filters

In the expense list of a group, you can filter by category...

20. Edit or archive an expense

Tap any expense in the list. The details open, with 2 buttons:

  • Edit: change description, amount, category...
  • Archive: the expense stops being part...

Archived expenses appear in a special button...

21. Archive and restore groups

Each group has an archive button...

Important: to archive a group you must first close its calculation.

To see archived groups, tap the Archived button...

There you can restore them or permanently delete them.

22. Delete a group

A group can only be deleted after archiving it...

Warning: deleting a group removes ALL its expenses...

If you want to keep the history, use archive instead of delete.

23. Install as an app on your phone

iPhone (Safari):

  • Open the app.
  • Tap the Share button (arrow up).
  • Choose "Add to Home Screen".

Android (Chrome):

  • Open the app.
  • Menu (3 dots) - "Install app".

It will look like a normal app, with its icon.
